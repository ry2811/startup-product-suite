# ReplyAI — Product case study

## Problem

Support agents spend too much time rebuilding context and writing repetitive responses while customers wait.

## Product decision

ReplyAI places the conversation, customer context and AI assistance in one workspace. AI drafts remain editable and are never sent automatically.

## Implemented experience

- Prioritized customer inbox and ticket switching.
- AI-generated conversation summary.
- Editable AI reply with visible generation state.
- Customer account and activity context.

## Role and stack

AI interaction design, B2B workflow design and frontend implementation using React, TypeScript and CSS.

## Suggested validation

Measure first-response time, editing effort and agent confidence against a workflow without AI assistance.
